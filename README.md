# 🌃 Night City Censor

[![Made with pollinations.ai](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pollinations/pollinations/main/packages/ui/src/brand/badge-made-with.svg)](https://pollinations.ai/?ref=badge)

**A steganography game where you out-scan an AI censor.**
Postcards arrive at your night-city desk — some carry a smuggler's hidden message in
plain sight, some are clean. Measure, count, decode, judge. Then the AI censor judges
the same pixels and you compare eyes.

Built for **POLLEN-QUEST #18367** (hidden messages in images), powered entirely by
**Pollinations LLMs** with **BYOP** (bring your own Pollen).

▶ **Play:** https://xiaotian1171.github.io/night-city-censor/

## How it works

| Mode | What happens | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Archive round** | 4 real cards generated by `gpt-6-astra` / `gpt-6-sol`, shipped with the AI censors' real verdicts recorded during development | free, offline |
| **Live round** | A Pollinations LLM smuggler hides fresh messages while you watch the SVG being drawn stroke by stroke; the AI censor then judges the rendered PNG pixels live | BYOP, ≈0.6–1.1 Pollen / round (Astra) |
| **Blueprint round** | Code-drawn night skylines and picket fences encode the message by construction (height = letter index; 5-picket binary). In testing, **4 different AI models missed every height skyline** — human home turf | free |
| **Smuggler desk** | Write a secret, let an AI smuggler (or a blueprint scheme) hide it, share a link that carries the whole picture in the URL | free / BYOP |
| **Specimen hall** | Encoding-family handbook with worked demos, Morse table, flip-to-reveal AI specimens | free |

Human vs AI scoring: +100 per correct clean/smuggled verdict, +150 exact decode
(+75 near-miss). The round ends in a copyable emoji scorecard.

## Pollinations integration

- **Endpoint:** `POST https://gen.pollinations.ai/v1/chat/completions` (OpenAI-compatible,
  CORS-open, called straight from the browser) — see `js/ai.js`.
- **Models:**
  - smugglers: `openai/gpt-6-astra` (default), `openai/gpt-6-sol` (master, subtler),
  - AI censors: `openai/gpt-6-luna` (rookie), `openai/gpt-6-sol` (veteran), `openai/gpt-6-astra` (legend).
- **Vision judging:** the app rasterizes each SVG to PNG on a canvas and sends *pixels only*
  to the censor — no metadata, no source, mirroring the "receiver sees a screenshot" rule.
- **BYOP:** `<polli-connect>` from `@pollinations/ui` (OAuth PKCE via
  `enter.pollinations.ai/authorize`), plus a paste-a-key fallback. The key lives only in
  `sessionStorage`. Paid-Pollen paywall responses are detected and explained in-app.
- **Free tier is fully playable:** archive rounds, blueprint rounds, the smuggler's
  blueprint schemes, the handbook and all tools need no key.

## Why the app is built this way

Inspired by the LessWrong post
*[How does Fable 5.1 hide messages in images?](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/WHEQps5PK8LrrcTAb)*
(Nikita Kezin, 13ryanC, hronir, James Chua, 2026-10-21). Its method — a fresh model session
sees only a screenshot and must find/decode the message — is implemented here literally:
the AI censor receives rendered pixels, and the human side gets a probe/ruler, a measurement
board and a clue handbook (height codes, Morse, color binary, counts, acrostics, decoys).

Two findings from that post drive the game design:

1. **Height encodings dominate** (33/50 messages; 23 were night skylines) — and in our tests
   every AI censor missed them. Hence the blueprint round: the human's guaranteed win.
2. **The decoder fits one rule at a time.** The handbook teaches the rule families one by one.

## Development notes

- All AI images were generated via Pollinations during development (2026-10-27); their
  recorded censor verdicts are in `assets/cards/records.json` and shown in-game as
  "recorded verdicts". With a key connected, the same cards can be re-judged live.
- `tests/test.mjs` (node) sanity-checks wiring: DOM ids vs `app.js`, i18n coverage,
  archive integrity. Run: `node tests/test.mjs`.
